# The Art of Nothing: 25 Years of Mutts and the Art of Patrick McDonnell

*A big landscape art book about the making of the "Mutts" comic strip, not a collection of strips.*

## In short

This is a 2019 Abrams ComicArts hardcover (240 pages) celebrating 25 years of Patrick McDonnell's comic strip *Mutts*. It's a **behind-the-scenes art retrospective**: sketches, process, unpublished work, commentary, a foreword by Eckhart Tolle, and an illustrated correspondence with Lynda Barry. **Verdict: worth it if you already love Mutts or enjoy cartooning craft. If you want the strips themselves, start with a regular Mutts collection.**

## Verdict

**Worth it for existing Mutts or cartoon-art fans. Skip it if you're a casual reader.** Reviews are mostly warm, with a few lukewarm ones. Your photo shows no price, so I can't say whether the sale price is good. *Confidence: medium.*

## Who it's for

- **Good for:** Mutts fans, aspiring cartoonists, and anyone who likes seeing how a strip goes from pencil to print. A nice gift for a Mutts fan.
- **Skip if:** you want to read the comics themselves, or you're new to Mutts. One reviewer suggests starting with a strip collection first.

## What's inside

- **Process and sketches:** roughs, sketchbook pages, proposals and unpublished artwork, plus published strips and paintings.
- **Commentary from McDonnell** about his influences and how he works, with some autobiographical notes.
- **Tolle's foreword** (listings also call it an introduction).
- **Format:** 240 pages, large landscape hardcover (about 12" x 9"), black-and-white and color.

**Flip-through (from reviews, not interior photos):** pages are built around process. Some Sunday strips are shown moving from **pencil to ink to print**, alongside sketchbook pages and paintings. It also covers his non-Mutts art (free-form pieces, tributes to artists who inspired him, and his animal-rights advocacy). The **closing pages are an illustrated correspondence with Lynda Barry**. I found no chapter list.

## Series and what comes next

**Stands alone, but it assumes you know Mutts.** It belongs to a long line of Mutts books:

- **Strip collections:** the first, *Mutts* (1996), through later treasuries such as *Everyday MUTTS* (listed as the 11th book).
- **Kids' books:** *The Mutts Diaries* and related titles.
- **McDonnell's separate storybook:** *The Gift of Nothing*.
- **Earlier art book:** Library Journal calls this a sequel to a 2003 monograph, but I couldn't confirm that earlier title.

If you're new to Mutts, a strip collection is the better first buy. The mutts.com store lists the full range.

## What people say

- **Positive:** "a mostly wonderful book about the career of MUTTS creator Patrick McDonnell"; praised for warmth, the animal-love in the work, and the look at McDonnell's mind. Library Journal calls it a lavish display of his art.
- **Mixed:** one reader found it "not much to brag about" and was put off by the eastern-mysticism tone. Another found it a bit tepid next to his other work.
