# Dbury@50: The Complete Digital Doonesbury

*A boxed set that is mostly a USB drive with 50 years of a Pulitzer-winning comic strip, not a book to read cover to cover.*

> **Test run of the V1 master prompt** on your bookstore photos. Searches were limited (see "What I couldn't verify").

## Verdict

**Worth it at the price you saw, if you're a Doonesbury fan with a Windows PC or a Mac that has a USB port.** The sticker shows **₱1,299 (50% off the ₱2,599 tag)**, which looks far below the US list price of $125 and is in line with the cheapest US prices I found. Skip it if you mainly read on a phone, tablet, or a MacBook without a USB port. *Confidence: medium-high on contents, medium on value.*

## What it is

I read the photos as the 2020 Andrews McMeel boxed set by G.B. Trudeau, ISBN 9781524861612, based on the cover, the back-cover contents list, the publisher logo, and the barcode. It's the **deluxe boxed set**, not a single book. Assumed locale: Philippines (₱ prices, National Book Store-style tags).

**Type of thing: a bundle / archive.** The "book" in it is a manual for the archive, not the main event.

## What's inside

Per the back cover and publisher/retailer listings:

- **USB flash drive** with all 50 years of Doonesbury (Oct 1970 to mid-2020), including **26 years of Sunday strips never before available digitally**, a searchable calendar archive, character bios, and a week-by-week description of the strip's contents. Comes with a woven lanyard.
- **"Dbury@50 User Manual"**, a 224-page wire-bound book walking through each year (about four pages per year, per the editor) with historical context, milestone strips and key storylines.
- **A 16" x 20" poster** with new drawings of the strip's 64 most prominent characters.

**Compatibility:** macOS 10.10+ and Windows 7+ laptops/desktops only. Not for phones, tablets, or most Chromebooks.

## What people say

I found only a little independent opinion:

- A **Jan 2021 blog review** calls it "highly attractive" as a retrospective but not especially insightful, and notes the package doesn't tell you which strips are the strong ones. It also says it's quite a bargain by some measures.
- **Buyer complaints** on retail pages: one had a MacBook Pro with no USB port; another said the program is lost when the drive is removed. A different buyer said it works well on a PC and the art looks clear.
- **Reddit:** I did not find any threads. This is a gap in the search, not evidence that nobody has discussed it.

## Price and where to buy

- **Your sticker:** ₱1,299 now, ₱2,599 before (the barcode label also shows ₱2,599). Roughly US$20-25 at recent exchange rates, which is my estimate.
- **US list price:** $125. One retailer listed $45.97 (out of stock). Used copies on eBay have sold for roughly $20-33.
- Several online sellers show it out of stock or on order, so the in-store copy may be a good find.
