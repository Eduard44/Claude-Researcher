# Dbury@50: The Complete Digital Doonesbury

*A boxed set whose real content is a USB drive holding 50 years of a Pulitzer-winning comic strip.*

## In short

This is a deluxe boxed set from 2020 by G.B. Trudeau (Andrews McMeel) celebrating 50 years of *Doonesbury*. The main item is a **USB flash drive with every strip from 1970 to 2020**, plus a wire-bound 224-page guide and a poster. It's an archive for fans, not a book to read through. **Verdict: a good buy at the ₱1,299 you saw, if you have a Windows PC or a Mac with a USB port.**

## Verdict

**Worth it at that price for a fan.** The sticker shows **₱1,299, down from ₱2,599**, and the US list price is $125, so this is a strong discount. Skip it if you'd mostly read on a phone, tablet, or a laptop without a USB port. *Confidence: medium-high on contents, medium on value (I couldn't check other Philippine sellers).*

## What it is

I identified it from the cover, the back-cover contents list, the Andrews McMeel logo, and the barcode (ISBN 9781524861612). It's the **boxed set**, not a single book. Assumed locale: Philippines (₱ stickers, National Book Store tags).

**Type: bundle / digital archive.** The printed book is a manual for the archive.

## What's inside

- **USB flash drive** with all 50 years of Doonesbury (Oct 1970 to mid-2020), including **26 years of Sunday strips never before digital**, a searchable calendar archive, character bios, and a week-by-week description of the strip. Sits on a woven lanyard.
- **"Dbury@50 User Manual"**: a 224-page spiral-bound book covering each year (about four pages per year, per the editor) with context, milestone strips and key storylines.
- **Poster**, 16" x 20", with new drawings of the strip's 64 most prominent characters.
- **Compatibility:** macOS 10.10+ and Windows 7+ laptops/desktops only. Not phones, tablets, or most Chromebooks.

**What it looks like open** (from the Amazon.com.au gallery you screenshotted): the box opens like a book. The left side holds a "Character Guide" sleeve with colored portraits of the cast, and the right side holds the spiral-bound User Manual with the drive and lanyard in a tray. That gallery has about 15 photos, including a laptop shot of the archive software. I couldn't embed them from here (see the end).

## What people say

- A **Jan 2021 blog review** calls it highly attractive but not especially insightful, and notes the package doesn't tell you which strips are the strong ones. It also says it's quite a bargain by some measures.
- **Buyer complaints:** one had a MacBook Pro with no USB port. Another said the program is lost when the drive is removed. Another said it works wonderfully on a PC and the art looks clear.
