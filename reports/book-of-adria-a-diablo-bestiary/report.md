# Book of Adria: A Diablo Bestiary

*Diablo's witch Adria "writes" a field guide to the monsters of Sanctuary, in a lore-and-art hardcover.*

## In short

This is an official Blizzard hardcover (2018) by Robert Brooks and Matt Burns, written in-universe as Adria's own notes on the demons, tribes and beasts of the Diablo world. It's a **lore and art reference book to dip into**, not a story or a game guide. **Verdict: worth it for Diablo fans, especially Diablo III players. Skip it if you want deep story or Diablo I/II coverage.**

## Verdict

**Worth it for a Diablo fan who likes artwork and lore.** Reviewers say it's less lore-heavy than the Book of Tyrael or Book of Lorath, and focused on Diablo III. *Confidence: medium (few independent reviews, no local price found).*

## Who it's for

- **Good for:** Diablo III players and fans of game art books who like a journal-style read. A solid gift for a Diablo fan.
- **Skip if:** you want a story you read straight through, or detail on Diablo I and II.

## What it is

I identified it from the cover (title, subtitle, Diablo logo). The photo is a cover image only, with no price or store tag, so I can't say which edition or where it was sold. It's the third book in Blizzard's Horadric Vault series. Published December 2018.

**Type: art / lore reference book (bestiary).**

## What's inside

- A **bestiary written as Adria's notes**: entries on corrupted beasts, tribes and demons across the franchise.
- It **opens with Adria's history in the Coven** (back to the Sin Wars), then moves toward the events leading up to Reaper of Souls. Some entries note which body parts can be used as reagents.
- **Page count is unclear:** listings give 144 or 244 pages. Check the publisher's listing.

**Flip-through (limited):** I found no chapter list or interior photos. Reader reviews describe **sketches throughout and brown-toned pages that give a journal-like feel**, and an early Blizzard preview showed penciled creature art with descriptions in Adria's voice. A retailer calls it "equal parts tome of lore and beautifully illustrated art book." Treat the layout as a reader's impression, not an official description.

## Series and what comes next

It's the **third of four Horadric Vault books**. Each covers a different topic, so you can pick one without reading the rest (my inference from the topics, not a source claim):

- **Book of Cain** (2011): the history of the war between Heaven and Hell, told by Deckard Cain.
- **Book of Tyrael** (2013): Sanctuary's history through the archangel's eyes.
- **Book of Adria** (2018): this one, a bestiary.
- **Book of Lorath** (2023): the magical relics.
- **Complete Collection** (Nov 2023): all four in one volume.
- **Book of Prava**: a separate book announced to come after the Horadric Vault. I found no release date.

## What people say

- **Diablofans** gave a positive review: if you liked the Book of Cain or Book of Tyrael you'll love this one, and the final section is stronger than anything in those books.
- **Buyers** praise the artwork and call it a good companion for Diablo III.
- **Critics:** a Goodreads reviewer said the switch to a bestiary lost what made the earlier books fun, and that there aren't enough entries to justify the lore. A buyer noted it's almost entirely Diablo III.
